2What I'll do

  • Plan, write, design, build and send four email campaigns a month to your opted-in list.
  • Set up the welcome email, at no extra cost, so every new newsletter sign-up gets it automatically.
  • Use your own photos, words, classes and prices. Where an email needs an image you don't have, I may create one from your real products, and you approve it before it's used.
  • Give every campaign tracked links, so we can see which emails and buttons people click.
  • Check your sending setup in GoHighLevel before we send more often, so the extra campaigns land in inboxes, not spam.
  • Send you a short results summary after each month: what went out, delivery and clicks for each email, and your online sales for the month against the same month last year. Open rates are shown but not relied on, because some mail apps open emails automatically.

4How we'll work together

  • Access. You give me sending access to your email platform and your online sales reports for November and December (the reports themselves, or a reporting-only login).
  • Approval. Nothing goes to your list until you've approved it in writing. A reply email saying "approved" counts, and so does the Approve button on your planning page. I'll send each campaign at least 2 business days before its send date.
  • Changes. Each campaign includes up to two rounds of changes.
  • Starting. The first November campaign goes out within 5 business days of me getting platform access and your first approval.

5November is on results

November is measured on the whole month. If your online sales for November 2026 don't beat November 2025 by more than my $750 fee, you don't pay for November.

Your baseline
  • November 2025 online sales: $70,288.70.
  • This comes from the daily sales report you sent on 5 November 2026: online class bookings, vouchers and packages, with private-class lump-sum payments removed.
  • This figure is fixed and won't be recalculated.
The target
  • November 2026 online sales must be more than $71,038.70 (last November plus $750).
  • If they are, November's $750 plus GST is payable. If they're $71,038.70 or less, November is free.
Example: if November 2026 online sales come to $73,500, that's more than $71,038.70, so November's fee is payable. If they come to $70,900, that's ahead of last year but not by more than $750, so November is free.
How November 2026 is measured
  • The same report and the same rules as the baseline: daily online sales from 1 to 30 November 2026, Adelaide time.
  • Everything in that report counts: class bookings, vouchers and packages, however the customer found you. Anything that isn't in the report, such as villa stays booked through a separate system, doesn't count in either year. Your booking system can't follow a click from an email to checkout, so we judge the whole month rather than each email.
  • The only exclusion is private-class lump-sum payments, as in 2025. Each one is listed with its date, amount and transaction number.
  • Refunds and cancellations of November sales are taken off, the same way they were for 2025.
How it's checked
  • By 3 December 2026 you send me the November daily sales report, plus your payment gateway's transaction report for the same dates so the two can be checked against each other. A reporting-only login to either is fine instead.
  • If the reports haven't arrived by 10 December 2026, the November fee is payable.
  • If fewer than three November campaigns go out because approvals or access were held up on your side, the results test doesn't apply and November is invoiced as normal.
  • This applies once, to November only, and needs a list of 1,000 or more opted-in contacts. You've told me you have around 29,400 contacts, and around 16,000 receive each newsletter.
December
  • December has no results test. In the final summary I'll show your online sales for 1 to 24 December 2026 against the same dates last year ($118,848.10).
